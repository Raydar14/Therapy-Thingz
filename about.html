Made by a therapist, for real humans.

Therapy Thingz is the home for everything Dr. Raychel Powers builds outside the therapy room so the tools reach the people who need them.

Dr. Raychel Powers, Psy.D.

Licensed psychologist and forensic evaluator based in Tallahassee, Florida.

Everything here is grounded in clinical work and shaped by real presence. The apps, the book, the courses, and the shop all come from the same belief: that healing happens in the present moment, and that the tools for it should be simple, honest, and made for real humans not perfect ones.

Therapy Thingz gathers it all into one place so you don't have to hunt for it. Take what helps. Leave what doesn't.
